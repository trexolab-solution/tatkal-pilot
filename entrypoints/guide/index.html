How to use TatkalPilot

A simple guide for your first Tatkal ticket. Read it once. It takes about 10 minutes.

Your privacy. This guide page is only text and drawings stored inside the extension. It never sends your data anywhere. It does not load anything from the internet.

1What TatkalPilot does

TatkalPilot is a Chrome extension. It helps you book a Tatkal ticket on the IRCTC website. It types the boring parts for you, so you are faster.

Tatkal: a special quota for last-minute travel. Its tickets open for booking only one day before the journey. They sell out very fast.

Quota: a group of seats kept for one kind of passenger. TatkalPilot always uses the Tatkal quota.

TatkalPilot does this

  • Fills From, To, date and Tatkal quota.
  • Searches trains. It can wait and search at the exact Tatkal opening time.
  • Finds your train and opens its seat availability.
  • Marks your class with an orange box, so you press BOOK fast.
  • Adds your passengers, chooses payment type and fills your options.
  • Tries again by itself when IRCTC is slow.

TatkalPilot never does this

  • It never logs in for you. It never sees your password or OTP.
  • It never solves the captcha. You type the captcha.
  • It never presses Pay & Book. It never touches the payment step. You pay.
  • It never works outside the IRCTC website.

TatkalPilot is not made by IRCTC or Indian Railways. Use it as IRCTC's rules allow. It cannot promise you a seat.

The whole job in 3 steps

  1. Save your booking once.

    Train, date, class and passengers.

  2. Log in to IRCTC yourself.

    Then press Start in the small TatkalPilot box on the page.

  3. You do the key steps.

    Press BOOK, type the captcha, and press Pay & Book.

2Before you start

Do these 3 things first. Do them the day before, not at 9:59 in the morning.

  1. Log in to IRCTC in Chrome.

    Open irctc.co.in and sign in as you always do. TatkalPilot will not log in for you. If you are logged out, it will not start.

  2. Keep the IRCTC tab open.

    TatkalPilot works inside that tab. Do not close it. Do not log out.

  3. Save passenger names in your IRCTC "Master list".

    On IRCTC, open your account and add the people who travel with you. This saved list is called the Master list. TatkalPilot picks names from it.

    You can also type a new passenger inside TatkalPilot. A saved name is simpler.

Tip. If the extension was just updated or reloaded, reload the IRCTC tab once (press F5). An old open tab can stop working after a reload.

3Make a booking

A "booking" here means your saved plan: which train, which day, which people. You can save many bookings.

Open the booking form

Click the TatkalPilot icon in the Chrome toolbar. A small box opens (the popup).

The form opens in a new tab. It has 3 parts: Journey, Passengers and Booking options. When you finish, press Save changes at the bottom. Chrome keeps it on your computer.

Help on every field. Click the small ? next to a name to read what it means. With the keyboard, Tab moves from one field to the next, and F1 shows the help for the field you are on. Press Esc to close it.

Part A. Journey

  1. 1 Booking name optional

    A name for you to find this booking later, for example "Home trip". If you leave it empty, TatkalPilot names it from the train, route and date.

  2. 2 Train number or name needed

    Type the 5-digit train number (like 12004) or part of its name. A list of trains appears. Pick one. The train name shows under the box.

    What to choose: the exact train you want. Check the number on IRCTC if you are not sure.

  3. 3 From and 4 To needed

    The station where you get on, and the station where you get off. Type the name or the short code (like NDLS). Pick from the list. The small swap button between them changes From and To.

    What to choose: the same stations you would type on IRCTC.

  4. 5 Journey date needed

    The day you will travel. Not the day you book. The buttons Today, Tomorrow and In 2 days are shortcuts.

  5. 6 Class needed default: 3A

    The kind of coach. Your choices are:

    • 1A: First AC
    • 2A: Second AC (2 tier)
    • 3A: Third AC (3 tier)
    • 3E: Third AC Economy
    • SL: Sleeper (no AC)
    • CC: AC Chair Car
    • EC: Executive Chair Car
    • 2S: Second Sitting (no AC)

    What to choose: only a class that your train really has. If the class is missing on the train, TatkalPilot stops and tells you which classes it saw. The class also decides the Tatkal opening time (see part 6).

  6. 7 Search at Tatkal opening time on for a new booking

    This is also called auto start. When it is on, TatkalPilot fills everything first and then waits. It presses Search at the exact Tatkal opening time. It checks IRCTC's own clock so it starts on the right second. When it is off, it searches as soon as you press Start.

    The time is set for you from your date and class (10:00:00 or 11:00:00). To search at another time, press Change time and type it as hours:minutes:seconds, for example 10:00:00.

    What to choose: keep it on when you book at the opening time. Turn it off only when Tatkal is already open and you want to search now.

Part B. Passengers

Press Add passenger for each person. You can add up to 4 people. This is the Tatkal limit for one ticket. For each person, choose one of two ways:

  1. 1 Master list default

    Use this for a person already saved in your IRCTC Master list.

  2. 2 The name box

    Type the name. A full name or only a part is fine, for example "Ravi". If two saved people match, TatkalPilot shows a small box on the IRCTC page and you pick the right one. If nobody matches, it tells you.

    What to choose: type enough of the name so only one person matches.

  3. 3 New passenger

    Use this for a person who is not in your Master list. Then fill these boxes:

  4. 4 Full name needed

    Name as on the ID. IRCTC allows only 16 letters, so a counter shows how many you used.

  5. 5 Age needed

    A number from 1 to 125.

  6. 6 Gender default: Male

    Male, Female or Transgender.

  7. 7 Country default: India

    Leave it as India unless the person is from another country.

  8. 8 Berth preference default: No Preference

    The seat you would like: No Preference, Lower, Middle, Upper, Side Lower or Side Upper. It is only a wish. IRCTC may give a different berth.

    What to choose: Lower for older people. Otherwise No Preference gives you the best chance.

Part C. Booking options

  1. 1 Payment default: BHIM/UPI

    How you plan to pay. TatkalPilot chooses this on IRCTC before the fare is counted, because the fee depends on it. UPI has the lower convenience fee. The other choice is Cards / Net banking. You still pay yourself.

    What to choose: BHIM/UPI if you use UPI. Otherwise Cards / Net banking.

  2. 2 Book only if a confirmed berth is available default: on

    A confirmed berth means a sure seat (status CNF). Other tickets are waitlist (WL, you wait for a cancellation) or RAC (you may only get a shared seat). When this is on, TatkalPilot ticks this choice on IRCTC, so you are not booked on a waitlist or RAC ticket. When it is off, TatkalPilot leaves the IRCTC choice as it is.

    What to choose: keep it on if you only want a sure seat.

  3. 3 Consider for auto upgradation default: off

    Auto upgradation lets IRCTC move you to a higher class for free when seats are empty, for example from 3A to 2A. It is not promised. When this is on, TatkalPilot ticks the choice on IRCTC. When it is off, it leaves it as it is.

    What to choose: on, if you are happy in a higher class. Off, if you want only the class you picked.

  4. 4 Also press Calculate Fare and Continue To Payment default: off

    When it is off, you press Calculate Fare yourself. When it is on, TatkalPilot presses Calculate Fare and then Continue To Payment for you. It stops on the payment page. It never presses Pay & Book.

    What to choose: off while you are learning. Turn it on later to save a few seconds.

  5. 5 More options all optional

    Press More options to open it. All three boxes can stay empty.

    • Mobile number: 10 digits. IRCTC sends ticket details here. If you leave it empty, IRCTC uses the number saved in your account.
    • Preferred coach: a coach name like B2, up to 4 characters. It is a wish only. IRCTC may not follow it.
    • GST number: 15 characters. GST means Goods and Services Tax. Fill this only if a company needs a tax invoice for this ticket. Most people leave it empty.

Save before you leave. Press Save changes. If the form is not complete, TatkalPilot shows what is missing: a 4 or 5 digit train number, From, To, date, class and at least one passenger.

The Duplicate button copies a booking. It is useful when you book the same trip for another day. Delete removes a booking.

4The Settings page

Press Manage in the popup, then open the Settings tab at the top. You can use TatkalPilot without changing anything here. The defaults are fine for most people.

Stations

It shows how many stations are saved. A built-in list is already inside the extension, so it works offline. TatkalPilot also refreshes the list from the internet once a day. Press Update now to refresh at once. If an update fails, it keeps using the saved list.

Trains

It shows how many trains TatkalPilot remembers. It learns train names from the IRCTC train list while you use IRCTC, and it also has a built-in list. Press Clear cache to forget the trains it learned. You can use this if a train name looks wrong. It is safe.

RailCore key optional

A key is like a small password for a free service called RailCore. With a key, the train box in your booking can search live trains. Without a key, everything still works. Press RailCore key to open the box, paste the key and press Save. Remove deletes it. A link there helps you get a free key.

Not sure? Skip the key. You never need it.

Retry & timing

Sometimes IRCTC is too busy and shows a "High Load" message. A person would press the button again. TatkalPilot does the same, a limited number of times. These settings decide how long it tries. They are used for every booking.

The 3 presets

  • Normal: the tested defaults. Use this on most days.
  • Heavy load: the same speed, but it keeps trying for 90 seconds instead of 60. Use it on very busy mornings.
  • Custom: your own numbers.

Updates

TatkalPilot is not on the Chrome Web Store. So it looks at GitHub once a day to see if a new version is out. Press Check now to look right away. If a new version exists, a Download button appears. The popup shows an "Update available" link, and the toolbar icon shows a small NEW mark. Nothing is installed for you. See part 7 for the update steps.

5On the IRCTC site

Open irctc.co.in and log in. A small blue box called TatkalPilot appears at the bottom right of the page. This is the floating panel. You press Start here, not in the popup.

If you are not logged in, the panel shows "Login required" and an Open login button. There is no Start button until you log in.

No booking saved yet? The panel says so, and the button says Create booking. It opens the booking form.

The 4 steps of a run

Press Start. TatkalPilot goes through 4 steps. At each step, some work is done for you and some work is yours.

  1. Search Fills From, To, date and Tatkal. Presses Search. If you chose a time, it waits and presses Search at that time. TatkalPilot
  2. Train Finds your train, opens availability and marks your class with an orange box. You press BOOK
  3. Passengers Adds passengers, chooses payment, fills your options. You press Calculate Fare
  4. Review Check the fare and details. Optionally it presses Continue To Payment for you. You do captcha and Pay & Book

Step 1. Search

TatkalPilot checks you are logged in. It fills the journey and the Tatkal quota. Then it presses Search. If you turned on "Search at Tatkal opening time", the panel says "Filled. Searches automatically at 10:00." and waits. If IRCTC does not open the train list, it presses Search again for a while, as set in Settings.

You: wait. Do not click on the page.

Step 2. Train

It looks for your train number in the list. It presses Check Availability, again and again if needed. Then it draws an orange box around your class and shows a message "press BOOK now".

You: press BOOK inside the orange box. TatkalPilot does not press it. If IRCTC resets the list after the box appears, TatkalPilot opens availability again and marks your class again, so you need only one press.

If BOOK is not there, Tatkal may not be open yet.

Step 3. Passengers

On the passenger page, TatkalPilot adds your Master list people, then your new passengers. It sets the mobile number, payment, and the options you chose. A message says "Passenger details filled. Press Calculate Fare." If some detail failed, the message tells you what to fix by hand.

You: look at the names and details. Then press Calculate Fare, unless you turned that option on.

If a page was already open, press Fill passengers on this page in the panel to fill it again.

Step 4. Review

The panel says "Check the fare, then continue." If you turned on the Calculate Fare option, TatkalPilot has pressed Calculate Fare and then Continue To Payment, and it stops on the payment page. If the option is off, it does nothing more.

You: read the fare. Type the captcha. Press Pay & Book and pay. This is always yours.

Helpful extras. TatkalPilot closes the tutorial pop-ups that IRCTC shows. When IRCTC says "Please confirm the booking details" because the train starts at a different station, it presses Continue Booking and tells you the station. If IRCTC asks "proceed booking without any preferences?", it does not answer. It tells you to press Yes or No yourself.

Stop, Restart and Edit

  • Stop ends the run now. You can also press Stop run in the popup. A run also stops by itself if you log out of IRCTC.
  • Restart starts again from the beginning. Use it after an error message.
  • The Edit pencil opens your booking to change it. Save the change, then press Start again. You cannot change the booking list box while a run is going.

6Tatkal timing tips

Tatkal tickets open one day before the journey date. The time depends on the class you choose.

Example. You travel on 25 November in 3A. Tatkal for 3A opens on 24 November at 10:00. For Sleeper (SL) on the same trip, it opens on 24 November at 11:00.

Tips

  1. Let TatkalPilot choose the time.

    With "Search at Tatkal opening time" on, it sets 10:00:00 or 11:00:00 from your class and date. You see the day and time next to the switch.

  2. Check your computer's date and time.

    The opening time follows your computer's clock. Set it to Indian time (IST). When the search runs, TatkalPilot also measures IRCTC's own clock, so the press lands on the right second.

  3. Press Start a few minutes early.

    For example 5 minutes before. Be logged in first. Do not press Start hours early. A run that waits more than about 30 minutes can expire, and then you must press Start again.

  4. If the time has already passed,

    TatkalPilot does not wait. It searches at once.

  5. Keep watching the orange box.

    When it appears, press BOOK quickly.

  6. Watch the timer.

    The popup and the panel show "Opens in …", "Tatkal open" or "Journey date passed" for each booking.

The times above come from TatkalPilot's own rule: AC classes at 10:00 and the others at 11:00. If IRCTC changes its timing, set your own time with Change time. Nobody can promise a confirmed seat.

7If something goes wrong

Press a question to open the answer.

IRCTC says "High Load" or looks stuck

This means IRCTC is too busy. It is normal at Tatkal time. You do not need to do anything first. TatkalPilot presses the button again by itself, like a person would, but only for a limited time.

  • The panel shows lines like "Check Availability: attempt 3 (retrying up to 40s)…" or "Searching again (#2, up to 50s left)…".
  • If it still fails when time is over, a red message appears. Then press Start or Restart to try again.
  • On a busy day choose the Heavy load preset in Settings. It keeps trying for 90 seconds.
  • Do not set the pauses very short. That can get your account slowed down.
Availability does not load, or the train is not found
Availability did not load after … attempts … Press Start to try again.
  • Press Restart in the panel.
  • If the message says the train was not found in the list, check that the train runs on that date and between those stations. Open Edit and check the train number, From, To and date.
  • If it says Class … not found on train …, the train has no such class. It shows the classes it saw. Change the class and save.
  • If it says "Availability keeps resetting. Please check it manually.", IRCTC has reset the list many times. Open availability yourself and press BOOK.
  • If it says "BOOK is not available yet (Tatkal not open?)", wait for the opening time and then press Restart.
"You are not logged in" or the Start button is missing
You are not logged in. Log in to IRCTC, then press Start.
  1. Log in on the IRCTC page. You can use the Open login button in the panel.
  2. Wait until the panel badge says Logged in.
  3. Press Start.

If IRCTC logs you out in the middle of a run, TatkalPilot stops the run on purpose.

"Complete the booking first" or the booking says Incomplete

Something needed is empty. The message lists it. Press the pencil (Edit), fill the missing item and press Save changes. A booking needs a 4 or 5 digit train number, From, To, a date, a class and at least one passenger.

The popup says the IRCTC tab needs a reload

This happens after the extension was updated or reloaded. Press Reload IRCTC tab in the popup, or press F5 on the IRCTC page. If no IRCTC tab is open, press Open IRCTC website. If you are on another tab, press Go to IRCTC tab.

A passenger was not added
  • "No passenger matching … in your IRCTC master list": the name is not saved on IRCTC. Add that person in your IRCTC Master list, or use New passenger.
  • "Several saved passengers match": a small box opens on the IRCTC page. Pick the right person.
  • Add the names in the Master list one day before, not at Tatkal time.
An update is available. How do I install it?

You see a NEW mark on the toolbar icon, an "Update available" link in the popup, or a Download button in Settings. Because TatkalPilot is not on the Chrome Web Store, you install the update by hand. It takes 2 minutes.

  1. Download.

    Press the Download link. It opens the Releases page on GitHub. Download the file named tatkal-pilot-…-chrome.zip. Only download it from that link.

  2. Unzip over the old folder.

    Unzip the file into the same folder you used before. Choose to replace the old files.

  3. Press Reload.

    In Chrome, type chrome://extensions in the address bar. Find TatkalPilot. Press the Reload (round arrow) button on its card.

  4. Reload your IRCTC tab.

    Press F5 on the IRCTC page.

Use the same folder. Chrome knows the extension by its folder. A different folder may be treated as a new extension, and your saved bookings may not come with it. To be safe, check your bookings after the update. The version number is at the bottom of this page and on the Settings page.

First-time install
  1. Download the zip from the Releases page and unzip it to a folder you will keep.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Press Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  4. Click the puzzle icon in Chrome and pin TatkalPilot, so it is easy to find.

Remember. Login, captcha and Pay & Book always stay with you. TatkalPilot only saves you typing.
